# Product Intelligence Update Inputs

Controlled import inputs for keeping Wingman current.

Rules:
- WyreStorm products can become recommendation-capable only after lifecycle, evidence and story checks.
- Competitor products are comparison-only fingerprints.
- Competitor products must not enter WyreStorm BOMs or proposal hardware lists.
- Do not feed live web data directly into recommendations.
- Every imported product needs an evidence source, reviewer and last reviewed date.
