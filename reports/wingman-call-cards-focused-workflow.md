# Wingman Live Call Cards Focused Workflow

## Purpose

This pass makes Live Call Cards work more like the primary live-call front door.

## Changes

- Keeps the current question and transcript capture visible.
- Collapses deeper Wingman interpretation by default.
- Keeps summary, missing detail, product direction and suggested shift available on request.
- Sends a structured call handoff into Discovery when the user opens Discovery.

## Rule

Live Call Cards should support one live sales conversation at a time:

- choose conversation type;
- capture the customer's wording;
- ask the next question;
- hand off to Discovery, Finder, Compare or Proposal.
