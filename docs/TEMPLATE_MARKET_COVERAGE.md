# Template market coverage review — 6 October 2026

The built-in catalogue now contains 75 room concepts across 15 vertical markets. Every vertical offers at least three distinct applications. Custom is a user-authored collection rather than a vertical, so it can legitimately have zero saved templates.

New designs use the established deployment/BOM generator and explicit source/output schedules. Local HDBaseT, matrix and camera-bridge arrangements are sized to the existing reviewed hardware profiles. Standard image assets are assigned explicitly; artwork illustrates an application and does not establish equipment suitability.

Every new template has an authored customer requirement, situation, three room-use moments, three acceptance outcomes and two customer decisions. These travel into overview and proposal content. Specialist interfaces, third-party supply, actual cable runs, signal formats and room conditions require project confirmation.

Industrial options are for advisory information and clean, non-hazardous AV locations. They do not connect AV routing to process or machine control. Transport displays remain separate from signalling and emergency communications. Broadcast review excludes calibrated mastering; spectator esports distribution excludes player monitors.

Application coverage was informed by the manufacturer's [market applications](https://videos.extron.com/company/markets/index.aspx), including energy operations, production, sports and broadcast, and [worship applications](https://www.extron.com/article/houseofworship). Hardware follows the existing local design profiles rather than equipment in those reference installations.

Validation: 48 catalogue/deployment/story/profile/schematic tests and 5 market-filter UI tests pass. All 75 template graphs are included in the schematic audit. Strict full-source typecheck, production build and sales-facing language check pass. Energy and Manufacturing options and all market counts were verified in the browser. The broader legacy custom-template test suite has four navigation/mode failures from its earlier flow; that test harness was not changed in this coverage work.
