# Known limitations for trusted testing

Wingman is not yet a final quoting tool.

Known limitations during trusted testing:

1. Product recommendations are product-direction guidance, not final engineered design.
2. Complex systems still require pre-sales review.
3. Product data may be incomplete or awaiting lifecycle review.
4. Competitor comparisons depend on available public/profiled data.
5. Proposal outputs must be reviewed before customer use.
6. Missing information should be treated as a blocker, not ignored.
7. AV-over-IP, USB, audio, control and network dependencies must be checked carefully.
8. Do not treat a product match as a direct replacement unless the evidence clearly supports it.
