# Suggested message to internal trusted testers

Subject: Wingman internal test access

We are ready to start a controlled internal test of WyreStorm Wingman.

Please treat this as an early internal sales and pre-sales assistant, not a finished quoting or design tool.

The aim is to check whether Wingman helps with:

- asking better customer questions;
- identifying the likely room/application type;
- choosing a safer WyreStorm product direction;
- spotting missing information;
- preparing customer-safe first-pass wording;
- knowing when to escalate to pre-sales.

Please use realistic opportunities and record anything that looks wrong, unclear, over-confident or commercially unsafe.

Key point: do not treat Wingman output as quote-final. Complex projects still need normal review.
