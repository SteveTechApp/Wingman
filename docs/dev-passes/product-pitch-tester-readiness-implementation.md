# Product Pitch tester-readiness implementation pass

## Goal

Make Product Pitch safe and useful for trusted internal testing.

Product Pitch should help a sales user explain a WyreStorm product direction, but it must not sound like a final engineered design or quote.

## Required Product Pitch structure

Product Pitch should clearly show:

1. Customer requirement
2. Product direction
3. Suggested system shape
4. Why this fits
5. What to check before quoting
6. Alternatives
7. Customer-safe wording
8. Internal guidance
9. Escalation / pre-sales review warning

## Safety rules

Product Pitch must not:

- present incomplete requirements as quote-final;
- hide assumptions;
- claim direct equivalence without evidence;
- ignore USB, audio, control or network dependencies;
- recommend accessories as lead products unless explicitly relevant;
- omit pre-sales review for complex systems.

## Trusted tester acceptance criteria

A tester should be able to answer:

- What is this product for?
- Why is Wingman suggesting it?
- What customer requirement does it address?
- What does it not cover?
- What must be checked before quoting?
- What can I safely say to a customer?
- When should I escalate to pre-sales?
